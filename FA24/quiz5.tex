\textbf{Note:} All solutions to ODE systems must be real (i.e. your answers
\textbf{shouldn't} have $i$ in them. In particular, a term of the form
$e^{i\theta}$ \textbf{shouldn't} appear anywhere).

\begin{enumerate}

\item Consider the following system of equations $x'=Ax$ where $
	A=\begin{pmatrix}
		-2 &  1 & 0\\
		 1 & -2 & 0\\
		 0 &  0 & 1\\
	\end{pmatrix}
$.

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Find the eigenvalues of A.
	\item Find the associated eigenvectors.
	\item Find the general solution of the ODE system.
\end{enumerate}

\item Consider the following system of equations $x'=Ax$ where $
	A=\begin{pmatrix}
		 3 & -1\\
		 1 & 1\\
	\end{pmatrix}
$.

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Find the eigenvalues of A.
	\item Find the eigenvectors eigenvectors.
	\item Find the particular solution satisfying the initial condition $x(0)=\begin{bmatrix}
			2\\
			1\\
	\end{bmatrix}$.
\end{enumerate}

\item Consider the following system of equations $x'=Ax$ where $
	A=\begin{pmatrix}
		 1 & -1\\
		 1 & 1\\
	\end{pmatrix}
$.

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Find the eigenvalues of A.
	\item Find the eigenvectors eigenvectors.
	\item Find the general solution of the ODE system.
\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
